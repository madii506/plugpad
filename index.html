How it works

One show. One fund. One read at a time.

The pairing is written into the coin's own description line, so anyone can read it on pump.fun without asking us.

01

Pair the coin with a show

Paste a podcast, a channel or a handle above. The description line becomes plugs <show> via PLUG. That line is the whole pairing. It never changes.

02

The fee fills the fund

100% of the creator fee accrues to that coin's fund. Not a pool, not a treasury, not a buyback. When the fund covers one read at the show's rate, a booking goes out at that rate, by the same email every other sponsor sends.

03

The host reads. The clip is the receipt.

The read airs in their normal episode. PLUG posts the clip, the timestamp and the episode link. Then the fund starts from zero and waits for the next read.

Board

Every coin, its show, its fund, its clips.

The board lists bookings and declines with their dates, and links every clip. It shows a dash where no number exists yet. A dash means no data. A zero means we measured and found nothing.

Limits

What it cannot do, so what it does.

It cannotSo it
make a host say yespublishes every booking and every decline, with dates, contact details removed.
pay a show from a smart contractnames the person who sends the booking and pays the invoice, and shows the invoice.
verify that anyone listeneddoes not count listens. It links the episode and the timestamp and lets you press play.
buy half a readwaits until the fund covers a whole one at the show's rate. The wait is visible on the board.
re-pair a coindoesn't. A stuck fund stays on the board as stuck. It is never pointed somewhere else.
read every platform from a serversays so on the card. Kick refuses server reads today; the pairing line still holds.
Words

A small dictionary, printed.

plug
One sponsor read, bought at the show's rate.
fund
One coin's accrued creator fee. Spent only on plugs for that coin's show.
rate
Whatever the show charges any sponsor. Never negotiated down, never paid over.
booking
The email, published with its date.
clip
The receipt: the episode link and the timestamp where the ticker is said.
decline
A published refusal. The fund stays where it is.
board
Every coin, its show, its fund, its clips. A dash where there is no data.
the line
plugs <show> via PLUG, in the coin's description. The whole pairing.
